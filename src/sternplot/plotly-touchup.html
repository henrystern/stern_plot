Plotly Touchup

Drop a Plotly figure here

Export one from Python with fig.write_json("figure.json"), then drop the file, paste its JSON, or use Open figure.
Click titles, legend names and annotations to edit them. Drag to move them.

Select annotations, shapes and images from the list. Click annotations and shapes in the figure, and drag an image to move it or its handles to resize it (Shift flips the aspect lock). Ctrl/Cmd-click adds or removes one, Shift-click selects a range, and edits apply to everything selected. Draw shapes with the toolbar.

Click an entry to jump back to that state.
